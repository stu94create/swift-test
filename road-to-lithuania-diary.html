Journey route

One stop per line, in travel order. Format: name, latitude, longitude, transport to get there — for example “Dublin, 53.35, -6.26, bus”. Latitude, longitude and transport are optional. The route map, moving transport icons and progress strip are built from this list, and each scene’s progress is worked out automatically from its location, from and to fields.

Save, load and share

Your project auto-saves in this browser. Export gives you a JSON file you can keep or reuse as a template for the next episode.

Scenes

Each scene has a start and end time in seconds (decimals allowed, e.g. 14.7). While the audio plays, the matching scene is shown automatically. Use “Preview this scene” to jump the audio to a scene.
