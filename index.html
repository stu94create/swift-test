          Assessment framework

          How the process works

          Every nomination follows the same five-stage assessment pathway. Cases progress only when the requirements of the preceding stage have been satisfied.

          1. Stage 1

            Public nomination

            A nomination is submitted with a description of the candidate’s act and any available supporting information.

            Median submission time: 4 minutes.

          2. Stage 2

            Initial assessment

            The registry evaluates whether the act meets minimum standards of exceptional everyday decency.

            Cases returned for clarification: 11%.

          3. Stage 3

            Witness verification

            Supporting accounts are reviewed where available.

            Average witness confidence: High.

          4. Stage 4

            Miracle assessment

            Where required, the case may proceed to preliminary miracle verification.

            Current backlog: approximately 8,421 cases.

          5. Stage 5

            External ecclesiastical consideration

            Eligible cases may be prepared for theoretical onward consideration.

            Current response rate from external ecclesiastical authorities: 0.00%.

          Documentation backlog: significant. The registry does not correspond with external authorities and makes no representation that any case will be considered by them.

              The registry

              About, methodology and access

              About the registry

              The Public Canonisation Registry is an independent record of publicly nominated acts of everyday decency. It was established on the principle that the ordinary conduct of ordinary people is documented far less carefully than their failures.

              The registry maintains candidate files, publishes weekly rankings, and prepares case documentation to a consistent standard. It holds no ecclesiastical authority of any kind and does not claim to.

              Methodology

              Nominations are weighted by the number of independent submissions received, the quality of supporting accounts, and the assessed level of public inconvenience prevented. Movement is calculated against the preceding reporting period.

              Where a candidate cannot be identified, the file is maintained under a description supplied by nominating parties. Candidate names are published in partial form as a matter of standing policy.

              Accessibility and privacy

              This site is built with semantic HTML, keyboard support throughout, visible focus indicators, and text alternatives for every statistic and ranking indicator. It respects reduced-motion preferences. If any part of it is difficult to use, that is a defect and not a design decision.

              No personal data is collected, stored or transmitted. There are no cookies, no analytics and no third-party services. Everything you enter stays in your browser and disappears when you close the page.
